Corporate Snakes and Ladders

Snakes and ladders for remote and hybrid teams.

Turn your company event into a live snakes and ladders race on everyone’s own phone. Every player gets their own board. Share one link. No app to download. Easy for you to set up, fun for every guest.

Why organizers choose it

1Ready in minutes. Pick the time, the size and the gifts. Start free, change anything until the game begins.
2Everyone plays on their phone. Guests open one link, type a nickname and get their own board. One board for each phone.
3Your message in the game. An opening, up to 3 moments during the game and a closing: a message, a video, audio, or a lucky draw.
4Fair for everyone. The software, not a person, decides the winners from public random numbers that many phones check.
5Your team can help. You, up to 5 Hosts and any number of Co-hosts. Only one payment is needed, from any one of you.
6A free AI helper. Paste your AI link into your own AI chat. It builds the draft. You press publish and pay.
7Players pay nothing. No real-money stake by players. No cash prizes. No betting. You give the gifts. We provide the software and never hold or pay prize money.
8Privacy that is easy to explain. Guest nicknames are erased within 24 hours after the game, and also when a game never starts. Designed to follow India’s DPDP Act, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA.

How it works

1Build your gamePick the time, size, gifts and turn length. It is a free draft until you publish.
2Invite your peopleShare one link in Slack, Teams, Zoom chat, e-mail or WhatsApp. Show the big screen in the room or on a video call.
3Pay once, then playPay once for the game, before it begins. Everyone throws the dice, and the software finds every winner.
