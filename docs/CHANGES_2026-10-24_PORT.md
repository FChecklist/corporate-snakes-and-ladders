# Changes of 2026-10-24 (ported from Corporate Tambola, adapted to this game)

- **What a person gives to get in.** Per game the organizer chooses `verify` = `mobile` (default), `id` or `both`, and `employeesOnly` (only the people on the list, no invitations, a mobile number or ID is always asked, never just a nickname). Code: `packages/beacon/src/config.ts`, `apps/edge/src/pii.ts` (sealed optional employee ID), `people.ts` (`normEmp`, `empHashOf`, `verifyOf`, `addPeople`, the export gets an Employee ID column only when `verify` is not `mobile`, `addWaiting`), `worker.ts` (join codes `need-id` and `bad-id`; an ID that is not on the list goes to the waiting room), `lifecycle.ts`.
- **Nickname joins.** This game lets a guest join the common link with a nickname. Once `verify` is `id` or `both`, or employees only is on, a nickname alone is refused and the join screen asks for the ID and/or the mobile number.
- **Console.** Step 2 has "Only my employees" and "What must a person give to get in?"; the invitation and the open-link choices are hidden while employees only is ticked. A small hint sits under every group and tick box, each open section is tinted in its own colour, the AI work-link boxes are not shown. The people list takes an Employee ID, shows it in the rows and in the waiting list, and has an "Upload a PDF" way with a preview before anyone is added (pdf.js is vendored in `apps/web/public/vendor/pdfjs`).
- **Test sign-ins.** `demo1@xyz.in`, `demo2@xyz.in`, `demo3@xyz.in` with the code `123456` (`TEST_LOGINS` in `worker.ts`); no mail is sent. Empty the list to switch them off.
- **Time source.** `fetchRound` asks the next mirror after 1.2 s (or at once when one fails) and the first valid answer wins; the phone keeps the newest of its last five clock answers.
