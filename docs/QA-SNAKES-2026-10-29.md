# QA of Corporate Snakes and Ladders, 29 Oct 2026 (cloud QA engineer)

Branch `qa/snakes-cloud-2026-10-29`, cut from `boss/docs-2026-10-29` (= live commit d2a3ebd + the boss documents, commit 77714da). Nothing was deployed, merged, or pushed to any other branch. No secret is in any file.
Plain words throughout. "Model" = `tools/qa-model.mjs`. "Before" = commit 77714da, "after" = the head of this branch.

## 1. Summary

- The live game (TEST mode) had **two real money/security holes and one missing product feature**: anyone could sign in with the public demo logins and get a free 1,000-person game on a LIVE site; a customer could not make the free 10-person demo.
- The model played about 15,000 whole games (every board size, both finish rules, race / team / table, automatic and players-throw, 6 head-counts, 3 turn limits, 3 end-when-N settings, one prize per person on and off, 2 prize sets) and 4,116 boards (294 layouts). It found **5 engine defects** (a prize taken back, ranking and prize disagreeing, a prize lost, two crashes). All 5 are fixed, each with a test that failed before.
- Three independent adversarial reviews (security, correctness, wording) found more real problems, mostly in my own new demo code and in the older help texts. Those that were plain defects are fixed (rows marked "found by review" in section 4); the rest are in section 6 for the owner.
- **Boards are sound**: 4,116 boards, every one solvable, no square is both a snake head and a ladder foot, no chains, no loops, every board equally hard (checked by two independent methods).
- **Needs the owner**: 9 decisions in section 7 (the biggest: "one prize per person" should be ON by default per the rules; "game ends when N are home" against the prize list; what a team race gives by default; the Table game type is half built; limits on free demos).

## 3. The model (`tools/qa-model.mjs`)

Run: `bun run tools/qa-model.mjs` (6 to 10 minutes), `--quick` (under a minute), `--part=board|game|config|worker|wording`, `--exhaustive` (the whole product, about 230,000 games, over an hour), `--json=out.json`.
It uses the real engine files (board, play, race, table, game, config) and the real Worker on an in-memory database. Its expectations come from `docs/boss/RULES_snakesladders.md` and the owner's order, re-stated independently in the model (every pawn is re-walked with the rules text; the end turn and the order of "reach the end" prizes are worked out a second way). Verdict words: FAIL (wrong or crashes), GAP (rules say one thing, product another), NOT_LOGICAL (allowed but makes no sense), INCOMPLETE (a promised result is missing), GIBBERISH, NO_RESULT (finished with no answer).

## 7. Owner decisions (each with my recommendation)

- **D1 One prize per person default.** Rules say ON. Recommendation: ON for new games, and show the first prize winner as soon as known (today every prize waits until the game ends when it is on).
- **D2 Game ends when N players are home.** Recommendation: the game ends when the last "reach the end" prize is won (rules 2.3) and the setting goes away, so the prize list and the end can never disagree.
- **D3 Turn limit by board.** Recommendation: board 100 = 60 turns (as now), 144 = 80, 225 = 110, or keep only the 100 board.
- **D4 Team race prizes.** Recommendation: a team race always has "Team win" 1st to 3rd and nothing else; add the lines automatically when a team race is chosen.
- **D5 Table game.** Recommendation: remove it from the product (hidden anyway) or finish it; do not leave it half built.
- **D6 Free demo limits.** Recommendation: at most 3 active free demos per account; the publish mail only to the signed-in account's own address.
- **D7 Names and pricing copy.** Recommendation: "Free demo game" for the 10-person game, "Practice game" for the 70-second one; one sentence on the pricing pages: "Try a free game for up to 10 people first."
- **D8 Player wording.** Which wording wins for the 11 messages of RULES section 10 (the rules draft or what the phone says today).
- **D9 Dates.** Free demo can be set 30 days ahead like an unpaid game (done); paid games 365 days (unchanged).

## 10. Audits

Three independent reviewers (fresh agents with no access to my conclusions) reviewed the branch: security, correctness, wording. They found the items marked "found by review" in section 4 (credit and pay link on a demo, demo made from a published game, demo date window, demo pack size, test addresses at the start, prize-line order, "Make a copy", "Paid" on a demo, the late-move pop-up, a flaky test, 67 type errors, the model's blind spots). Their findings that I did not fix are in section 6 (U10 to U14). The correctness reviewer ran about 1,900 random games against the new engine rules and found no mismatch, and confirmed the bundle `zgame.js` equals a fresh build.
Model blind spots they named, and what I did: the end turn was never checked independently (added G-03b), the demo checks were happy-path only (added W-19 to W-21), two checks could never pass (removed), the demo proof reused the engine to recompute (now said plainly).
