How Corporate Snakes and Ladders plays

Race up your own board. Snakes pull you down, ladders lift you up.

Your board

10 x 10 squares

The classic board, squares 1 to 100.

9 snakes, 9 ladders

Different for every player.

Equally hard

A ladder lifts 8 to 30 squares and a snake drops 8 to 40. Each board is checked so nobody gets an easier one.

Secret until the start

Boards are drawn after sign-up closes, from a public random source. Nobody can know or choose one.

A turn

1

The window opens

You have 20 seconds to press the dice.

2

The window closes

Players who threw get a number. Others miss the turn.

3

The reveal

Everyone's dice shows. Every pawn moves on its own board.

4

Next turn

About 25 seconds a turn.

  • The dice are random. The number comes from a public random source published after your window closes, so nobody can know it in advance.
  • You need an exact roll to land on the last square.

Prizes

Milestone line

The first player to reach the milestone square (square 75 by default).

First to finish

The first five players to reach the last square win 1st to 5th.

Top ten

Places 6 to 10 are the best-placed players when the game ends.

Clear winners

Prizes are never shared. If players reach the same place on the same turn, the next measure decides and a random draw is the last measure. The results show how each tie was decided.

Two ways to play

Fast mode

Everyone's throw happens when the window closes. It works for thousands. Every phone shows the same crowd and the same winners.

Strict mode

A throw must be pressed inside the window. A missed press is a missed turn. This is for smaller games.
