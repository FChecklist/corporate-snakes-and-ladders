About Corporate Snakes and Ladders

Corporate Snakes and Ladders is made by IT Services by Rajat Agarwal.

Why Corporate Snakes and Ladders

Companies want events that bring people together online. They do not want an app to install or a game to explain.

So we make games everyone already knows. They run on the players' own phones and laptops. They work for a hundred people or for thousands.

Every Corporate Game gives a clear first, second, third, fourth and fifth place and a top-ten list. There are no ties.
