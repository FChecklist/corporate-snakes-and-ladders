Corporate Snakes and Ladders

The game everyone grew up with. Every player gets their own board. The dice roll by themselves every 20 seconds. Thousands race together.

How it playsTell me when it opens

How a game works

1

Get your own board

Your snakes and ladders are different from everyone else's. Nobody knows the boards before the game starts.

2

Dice roll by themselves

Every 20 seconds your dice roll and your pawn moves. Nothing to press.

3

Everyone moves together

One shared clock. The software checks every throw and moves every player.

4

Race to the top

The first to the milestone line wins a prize. The first five to finish win the big prizes.

Why companies like it

Fair boards

Boards differ, but every board is equally hard. We check each one.

See the crowd

See how many are playing and where everyone is on the board.

Truly random

Boards and dice come from a public random draw nobody can predict, not even us.

Clear winners

A strict 1st to 5th and a top-ten list. No ties.

Two ways to play

Dice that roll by themselves (the default, for thousands), or an option where players press the dice themselves in time (for smaller games).

Nicknames

Players use nicknames. Real names stay private.

Clear winners, always

1st to 5th, always

Every game ends with a clear first, second, third, fourth and fifth place.

A top-ten list

Places six to ten are listed too.

No ties

If players are level, the next measure decides. The last measure is a random draw anyone can re-check.

Told in one line

The results say how any tie was decided.

Questions

How many can play?

Thousands in the fast mode. The strict mode is for smaller games, about a thousand players.

How long does a game last?

With thousands of players the first finishers arrive in about five minutes. Bigger boards make it longer.

Is it really random?

Yes. Boards and dice come from a public random source, published only after the moment they are needed.

Do players need an app?

No. It runs in the browser.

Do players pay?

No. The organizer pays.
